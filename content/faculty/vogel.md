Professor Vogel received a BA in Psychology from University of Puget Sound and a Ph.D. in Psychology from University of Iowa. He completed his dissertation in the lab of Steven J. Luck. Professor Vogel held a postdoctoral research position at the Institute for Neural Computation at University of California, San Diego.

Prior to UChicago, Professor Vogel held faculty appointments in the Department of Psychology and the Institute for Neuroscience at University of Oregon.
