Professor Bakkour received a BS in Neuroscience from Brown University then conducted three years of translational research at Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston working with Dr. Bradford Dickerson developing imaging biomarkers of neurodegenerative disease. He completed his PhD in Neuroscience at The University of Texas at Austin in the lab of Dr. Russell Poldrack studying value-based decision making and behavioral change. He then completed postdoctoral training at Columbia University in New York working with Dr. Daphna Shohamy and Dr. Michael Shadlen before moving to Chicago. Professor Bakkour’s research focuses on the cognitive and neural mechanisms by which relational memory contributes to decision making using a combination of functional brain imaging, eyetracking, computational modeling, machine learning, and neuropsychological studies with patients that have known cognitive deficits.
