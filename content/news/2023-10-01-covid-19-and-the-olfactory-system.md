Professor [Leslie Kay](https://kaylab.uchicago.edu/) reviews COVID-19 infection via the olfactory system and the potential consequences of the body’s inflammatory response. She [discusses](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.00255.2022) the potential long-term implications of neurodegeneration observed in cortical areas that have connections with the olfactory system.
