 [Awh and Vogel Lab](https://awhvogellab.com/) develops a change localization task variant that provides a highly reliable and sensitive approach for measuring visual working memory capacity. [Zhao et al., 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13414-022-02586-0) shows the new task variant is far more reliable than the traditional change detection task, requiring only half the number of trials to achieve robust reliability.
