Research from the [London Lab](https://voices.uchicago.edu/london/) investigates changes in brain connectivity during the critical development period for song learning in the zebra finch. By using functional imaging, [Layden et al. 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053811920307047) identified relationships between functional connectivity changes in specific brain regions involved in auditory processing and behavioral changes during this period.
