# How to update the IMB website

This guide is for whoever looks after the Institute's website day to day. You don't need any technical knowledge. Everything is done by filling in forms in a web dashboard.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. You'll get an email invitation from **Pages CMS**. Pages CMS is the website's dashboard.
2. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org** and sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to.
3. Click the **IMB website** to open the dashboard.

Bookmark the dashboard page. You'll come back to it every time.

## 2. A quick tour

The menu on the left lists everything you can change:

| Menu item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **News** | Announcements, awards, new papers, welcome messages |
| **Events** | Talks, cookie hours, cookouts, meetings |
| **Publications** | Papers by Institute members |
| **People → Faculty** | Faculty members, with bio, photo, links and research areas |
| **People → Fellows** | Graduate students and postdocs |
| **People → Administration** | Institute staff |
| **Research areas** | The research areas listed under "Our Research" |
| **Page text** | The wording on the Home, About, Contact and other pages |
| **Contact details** | Address, contact email and phone number shown on every page |

**Saving publishes your change.** About **2 minutes** after you click **Save**, the public website updates by itself. There's no separate "publish" step.

## 3. Everyday tasks

### Post a news story
1. Click **News**, then **Add an entry** (or **New**).
2. Fill in the **Headline**, the **Date** and a **Category**.
3. Optionally, add an **Image**. Wide pictures look best.
4. Write the **Story**. The toolbar works like Word: bold, links, bullet points.
5. Optionally, add a one- or two-sentence **Short summary**. It's shown on the home page. If you leave it blank, the start of the story is used.
6. Click **Save**.

The four newest stories appear on the home page automatically.

**Tip:** Not ready to publish yet? Tick **Draft**. A draft is saved but stays hidden from the website. Untick it when you're ready.

### Add an event
1. Click **Events**. Each event is shown as one line (date and name); click a line to open it.
   To add an event, scroll to the bottom and click **Add an item**. New items appear at the bottom of the list, which is fine.
2. Fill in the **Event name**, **Date**, **Time** and **Location**.
3. Click **Save**.

You never need to sort events or remove old ones. The site shows upcoming events at the top, and moves each event to "Past events" automatically the day after it happens.

A good habit is to add a whole quarter's events at once, e.g. every Cookie Hour and ProSeminar for Winter quarter.

### Add a publication
1. Click **Publications**, scroll to the bottom and click **Add an item**.
2. Paste the paper's **DOI** into the DOI box. You can usually find it on the journal's page; it looks like `10.1177/09567976221090923`. You can paste the full `https://doi.org/...` link too.
3. Under **Institute members on this paper**, choose the faculty members who are authors.
4. Click **Save**.

That's all. The website looks up the title, authors, journal and year automatically. Leave the other boxes empty unless the paper has no DOI, in which case fill them in by hand.

### Welcome a new student or postdoc
1. Click **People → Fellows**, scroll to the bottom and click **Add an item**.
2. Fill in their **Full name**, **Role**, **Lab** and, optionally, a **Photo** and **Profile link**.
3. Click **Save**.

Fellows are sorted alphabetically by last name automatically. If there's no photo, the site shows the person's initials.

### Someone has left
Open the right section (Fellows, Faculty or Administration), find the person, and delete their entry. In Fellows and Administration, click the person's line to open it first. Then **Save**.

Each September, it's worth looking through the Fellows list for anyone who has graduated or moved on.

### Add a new faculty member
1. Click **People → Faculty**, then **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in their **Full name**, **Degree**, and their **Titles** (one appointment per line).
3. Upload a **Photo**, then add the **Lab website** and a link to their full publication list.
4. Choose their **Research areas**. They'll then appear on those research-area pages automatically.
5. Paste in their **Biography**.
6. Click **Save**.

### Change a photo
Open the person's entry, click the photo, and upload a new one. Use a square headshot that's at least 400 × 400 pixels.

### Edit the wording on a page
Click **Page text**, pick the page, edit the text, and click **Save**.

### Add a new research area
Click **Research areas**, add an entry with the area's name and a one-line summary, and **Save**. It appears in the "Our Research" menu automatically. Then open each relevant faculty member and add the new area to their **Research areas**.

## 4. Checking that everything worked

The website has a private status page: **your-website-address/site-status/**, for example `https://imb.uchicago.edu/site-status/`.

It shows:

- **When the site last updated.** If your change was saved after that time, wait a couple of minutes and refresh.
- **Anything that needs fixing.** For example, an event with a date the site couldn't read, or a DOI that didn't work. Everything else still updates normally. Only the item with the problem is skipped.

If it says **All good ✓**, you're done.

## 5. If something goes wrong

- **You can't break the website by mistake.** Every change is saved in a history, and any earlier version can be brought back. If you delete something by accident, ask for help and it can be restored.
- If a change hasn't shown up after 5 minutes, check the status page. You can also click **Publish site now** in the dashboard.
- For anything else, contact: **______________________** *(fill in who supports the website)*

---

## 6. A suggested routine

Keeping the site current takes a few minutes a month:

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Start of each quarter | Add that quarter's events |
| Each month | Post any news: awards, new grants, papers, visitors |
| When a paper comes out | Add it under Publications (just the DOI) |
| September | Add new fellows; remove those who have left |
| Once a year | Ask faculty to check their bio, titles and photo |
